Motorbikes & scooters

Biggest danger: No protection, and drivers often don't see you.

If you remember nothing else

  1. Wear a fastened helmet, every ride.
  2. Keep three seconds behind the vehicle ahead.
  3. Assume the driver hasn't seen you.

Try it

How far will you travel before you stop?

30-second check

  • Helmet on, strap fastened
  • Both brakes firm
  • Tyres: pressure and tread
  • Lights, indicators, horn
  • Gloves, jacket, closed shoes

Speed & braking

  • Squeeze both brakes, front slightly more.
  • Brake before the bend, not in it.
  • Take speed breakers slowly, bike upright.
  • At night, ride within your headlight's reach.

Position & visibility

  • Ride in the clean wheel track.
  • Stay out of truck and bus blind spots.
  • Headlight on, even by day.
  • Signal early, then shoulder-check.

Junctions & overtaking

  • Overtake on the right only, with a clear view.
  • Never weave between moving vehicles.
  • Stay off the footpath.
  • Expect cars from every gate and side road.

Rain, fog, night

Double your gap and brake earlier and gentler. In fog use low beam; if you can't see, pull off and wait.

Passengers & loads

One pillion, with a helmet and feet on the rests. Keep loads low and clear of wheels. Never ride tired or after alcohol.

Passenger cars

Biggest danger: Blind spots, tailgating and anything that takes your eyes off the road.

If you remember nothing else

  1. Belts on, front and back.
  2. Three-second gap, six in rain or fog.
  3. Shoulder-check before every lane change.

Try it

How far will you travel before you stop?

30-second check

  • Seatbelts on, everyone
  • Mirrors set, windscreen clean
  • Tyres and a working spare
  • Lights, wipers, horn
  • Phone away, route set
Go deeper: more habits2 min read

Speed & distance

  • Slow down in bazaars, near schools and at night.
  • Add gap on wet or foggy roads.
  • Expect pedestrians at crossings and stops.
  • Take speed breakers slowly.

Position & visibility

  • Check mirrors every few seconds.
  • Signal early. Hazards only when stopped.
  • Dip lights for oncoming, fog lights in fog only.
  • Check behind before reversing, especially for children.

Focus & fitness

  • No phone in your hand.
  • No driving tired or after alcohol.
  • Plan rest stops.
  • Secure loose items.

Fog, rain, floods

Use low beam in fog and slow right down. Never drive into flooded underpasses.

Kids & CNG

Children in the back, belted, child locks on. Never leave a child in a parked car. At CNG fuelling, engine off, follow the station's rules. Smell gas: stop, switch off, get out.

Auto rickshaws

Biggest danger: They roll over in sharp turns, and nothing protects the people inside.

If you remember nothing else

  1. Slow right down before every turn.
  2. Never carry more than the seat limit.
  3. Pull fully left before you stop.

Try it

How far will you travel before you stop?

30-second check

  • Brakes hold firmly
  • Tyres: pressure and tread
  • Lights and reflectors
  • Seats and handrails secure
  • No smell of gas
Go deeper: more habits2 min read

If you drive one

  • Keep both hands on the handlebar, except to signal.
  • Brake early and in a straight line.
  • Keep left. Don't cut across lanes.
  • Slow down for potholes instead of swerving.

If you are a passenger

  • Sit fully inside and hold the handrail.
  • Keep bags and arms inside.
  • Ask the driver to slow down if turns feel fast.
  • At night, share the number plate if you travel alone.

If you share the road

  • Give them room. They stop and turn suddenly.
  • Don't overtake close.
  • Watch for them coming out of side lanes.

Rain & night

Wet roads need slower speed and longer gaps. Check the tail light and reflectors before dusk.

CNG safety

Engine off when refuelling. If you smell gas, stop, switch off and move away from flames.

Vans, pickups & loaders

Biggest danger: A loaded vehicle stops slowly and tips easily, and loose loads or riders in the back can be thrown out.

If you remember nothing else

  1. Tie the load and respect the limit.
  2. Leave a bigger gap when loaded.
  3. Nobody rides in the open back.

Try it

How far will you travel before you stop?

30-second check

  • Load tied and within the limit
  • Overhang marked (cloth or light)
  • Tyres inflated for the load
  • Brakes firm
  • Doors locked (passenger vans)
Go deeper: more habits2 min read

Speed & braking

  • Leave extra distance whenever you carry weight.
  • Slow early for turns and junctions.
  • Use lower gears on descents.
  • Don't tailgate.

Loads & passengers

  • Never carry people in the open back.
  • Don't overload, even for a short trip.
  • Passenger vans: no standing, nobody on the roof.
  • Stop only at safe places.

Fitness & parking

  • Rest before long trips.
  • Use the handbrake, park on level ground.
  • Check behind before pulling away.
  • Engine off when loading.

Wet roads & fog

Leave a longer gap when loaded. In fog use low beam and never stop in a live lane.

School & passenger vans

Count seats and never exceed them. Doors closed and locked while moving. Children seated, bags stowed.

Buses & trucks

Biggest danger: Long stopping distance, wide turns and large blind zones. Fatigue and speed make it worse.

If you remember nothing else

  1. Never race, never stop mid-lane.
  2. Leave a much bigger gap than a car.
  3. Doors closed, nobody on the roof.

Try it

How far will you travel before you stop?

30-second check

  • Brakes and air pressure
  • Tyres and wheel nuts
  • Lights, mirrors, reflective tape
  • Doors and emergency exits
  • Driver rested
Go deeper: more habits2 min read

If you drive one

  • Rest properly and keep to driving-hours limits.
  • Check mirrors constantly.
  • Slow for bends, junctions and crowded stops.
  • Never exceed the load or passenger limit.

If you are a passenger

  • Wait for the bus to stop before boarding or leaving.
  • Hold on. Stay seated or behind the line.
  • Keep arms and bags inside, stay off the roof.
  • Know where the emergency exit is.

If you share the road

  • Stay out of the no-zones, especially the left side.
  • Pass quickly and cleanly. Don't cut in.
  • Keep back. If you can't see the mirrors, they can't see you.

Highway breakdown

Pull as far off as you can, hazards on, warning triangle well behind. Get everyone off the traffic side.

Night & fog

Check lights and reflective tape. Never park on the carriageway. If you must stop, hazards and triangles.

Five habits for every road user

  1. Look far aheadScan beyond the vehicle in front. Early warning means calm reactions.
  2. Keep your gapThree seconds in the dry. Double it in rain or fog.
  3. Be seenLights on, signal early, never assume you've been noticed.
  4. Slow before the hazardJunctions, bends, crossings, bazaars, speed breakers.
  5. Stay focusedNo phone in hand. No driving tired or after alcohol.
